Good questions

We want this to fit how trade businesses work — not add another admin job.

Does it automatically send quotes?

No. The workflow shown here keeps a person in control. The draft is reviewed and approved by the owner or authorised team member before it is sent.

Do we have to change how we work?

The aim is to fit around the information your team already captures. The exact setup would depend on your current job and quote process.

Will it know our prices straight away?

It needs your pricing rules and/or suitable historical quote data to make useful suggestions. The quality of the suggestions depends on the information available and how the system is configured.

Is this a finished product?

This page describes an early-stage concept and is intended to find contractor partners for discovery and testing. Features, integrations and availability are still to be validated.

What happens to our pricing data?

Pricing information is commercially sensitive. Any pilot should clearly agree what data is used, who can access it, how it is protected and how it can be removed before real business data is shared.
